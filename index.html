    Rules

    • Pocket all the balls; the black 8 must come last. If it goes in earlier, the game is lost.
    • Foul: if the cue touches a coloured ball before the white one, the game is lost at once.
    • The white ball is put back after it is pocketed.
    • No new shot while the balls are rolling.
    • You get thirsty and want to smoke; the cue sways unless you are calm (drinking and smoking move the bars). Drunk players aim less well.

    Controls

    • Drag the mouse: aim left/right, tilt the camera up/down. A / D or ← / → turn.
    • Mouse wheel, W / S or ↑ / ↓: power.
    • E or a click: shoot.
    • Drag the white dot above the SHOOT button: where the cue tip meets the ball (double-click: center).
    • R, hold: drink gulp after gulp.
    • T, hold: draw on the cigarette, release: exhale.
    • C: switch between the player camera and the free camera.
    • Free camera: W A S D (or the arrow keys) move, Space up, Shift down, mouse looks around (Esc frees the mouse).
    • Piano (free camera, close to it: F sits you down, Esc gets you up): the keys play it.
    • N: new rack.
